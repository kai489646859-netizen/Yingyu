# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Project participation should be respectful, constructive and focused on improving the software. Welcome people regardless of experience or background, discuss technical evidence rather than individuals, and respect privacy and intellectual property.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information, publishing credentials or media links, spam, and repeated disruption after a maintainer request.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect users and the project. Security or privacy-sensitive reports should use the private process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

When reporting conduct concerns, provide the relevant link and a short factual description. Maintainers will review the context and take proportionate action.
